BIDSENTRY
Bid portal watch service

Government contracts go to whoever sees the posting first.

State and local agencies post bids on dozens of separate portals, often with two weeks to respond. BidSentry watches the portals that carry your trade and sends you a sourced flag the day a matching RFP appears - with the deadline, the buyer, and the link to the original posting.

How it works

1. Tell us your trade"Commercial roofing, Texas" or "IT services, federal" is enough. We map it to the portals and commodity codes that actually carry those bids.
2. We watch the portalsState procurement sites, county and city bid boards, and SAM.gov - checked on a rolling schedule, not scraped once a month.
3. You get the flagA short, dated note: what posted, who is buying, when it closes, and the link to the source so you can verify every word.

Why not just check yourself

Because the postings are scattered across portals that each work differently, and the window is short. The businesses that win small government contracts are the ones with a routine for seeing everything - this service is that routine, run for you every day.
